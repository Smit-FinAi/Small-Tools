UAE Gold VAT Guide

Complete reference for Dubai Gold Souk traders — 2025/2026

✅ Reflects Cabinet Decision No. 127 of 2024, effective 25 February 2025. Consult a UAE tax consultant for your specific situation.
📋 Three VAT Scenarios for Gold
ScenarioGold TypeTransactionVAT RateWho PaysInvoice Note
1. Retail Jewellery22K/21K/18K jewelleryB2C (any buyer)5%Seller → FTAStandard Tax Invoice with TRN
2. Investment Gold≥99% purity (24K bars/coins)B2B (both VAT-reg.)0%Zero-rated"Zero-rated — Investment Precious Metal"
3. Reverse ChargeAny gold/jewelleryB2B (both UAE VAT-reg.)RCMBuyer self-accounts"VAT to be accounted by recipient under RCM"
📌 Key VAT Rules
  • 🟡 Standard VAT: 5% on jewellery
  • 🟢 Investment gold ≥99%: Zero-rated (0%)
  • 🔵 B2B VAT-registered: RCM applies
  • 📋 Mandatory TRN: turnover > AED 375,000
  • 📋 Voluntary TRN: above AED 187,500
  • 🏛️ Filing: Quarterly / monthly with FTA
  • 📅 Deadline: 28th of following month
  • 🏢 Corporate Tax: 9% on profits > AED 375,000
  • 🛂 Tourist VAT refund via Planet Tax Free
  • 💱 Min. tourist purchase: AED 250 per invoice
  • 📄 Records: min 5 years
🧾 Invoice Must Include (FTA)
  • 📝 Title: "Tax Invoice / فاتورة ضريبية"
  • 🔢 Sequential invoice number
  • 📅 Invoice date
  • 🏪 Seller name, address, TRN
  • 👤 Buyer name (B2B: buyer TRN too)
  • ⚖️ Item description, weight (g), karat
  • 💰 Rate per gram (AED)
  • 🔧 Making charges (separate line)
  • 💵 Taxable amount (ex-VAT)
  • % VAT rate applied
  • 💰 VAT amount in AED
  • 🧮 Total including VAT
  • 📋 RCM: "Reverse Charge applies"
